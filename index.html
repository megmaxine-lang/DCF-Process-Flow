Dynamic Cycling Fit · Data Governance Specification

DCF 資料治理、驗證與發布循環流程 / Governance, Validation & Release Process Flow Chart

適用於新車上市前及既有車款零件改版的資料準備、PIM 資料建立、有效騎乘幾何計算、API / App 雙層驗證、正式發布與閉環維護
Standard Operating Procedure for pre-launch bike and component revision data prep, PIM integration, fit geometry calculations, API/App testing, production sign-off, and closed-loop maintenance.

Trigger: Prior to new bike launch or component revision
